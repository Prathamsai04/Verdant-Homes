# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students and early-career professionals relocating to Bengaluru who compare homes on their phones, often during a commute or between classes. Property operators use the admin workspace to qualify enquiries, protect inventory, and keep public information current.

## Product Purpose

Verdant House makes premium shared living feel legible before a visit: honest room availability, clear inclusions, food and community details, and an enquiry journey that never pretends to be an instant booking. Success means a guest can confidently request a room in minutes and an operator can move that request to a confirmed stay without overbooking.

## Brand Personality

Cultivated, candid, quietly spirited. The experience should feel like stepping from a loud Bengaluru street into a shaded courtyard: composed, fresh, and unmistakably cared for.

## Anti-references

Do not copy MQBoysPG branding, language, layouts, or source. Avoid generic hotel gold, forest-green-on-cream luxury templates, corporate prop-tech dashboards, endless identical amenity cards, fake urgency, and “booking confirmed” language before human approval.

## Design Principles

- Make the room and its terms tangible before asking for contact details.
- Earn trust through operational clarity, not superlatives.
- Let one cultivated green identity carry the public experience.
- Keep guest flows calm and operator flows efficient.
- Treat every state—including empty, waiting, and unavailable—as useful information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard-complete navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive image text, reduced-motion behavior, non-color status cues, readable contrast, and form errors tied to their fields.
